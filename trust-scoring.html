Questions

Common questions.

Existing scoring systems measure human financial behavior or wallet patterns. Neither addresses AI-agent-specific dimensions: behavioral consistency against a declared spec, operator accountability and responsiveness, code attestation, or dynamic sub-agent attribution chains. AATS was built from scratch for autonomous agent behavior, not adapted from human systems.
At the exact moment a contract executes, both agents' complete trust states are locked to the tamper-evident ledger — scores, tiers, all subscores. Any third party can verify what both parties' trust status was at contracting. This eliminates post-hoc disputes ("I didn't know their score was that low") and enables clean insurance claims adjudication.
When a critical alert (fraud flag, insurance claim, OFAC match) goes unacknowledged for 48 hours, the Operator Responsiveness Flag is applied. A 5-point penalty hits all agents under that operator. A mandatory warning appears in all future trust reports. The flag is anchored to the ledger — publicly visible to any counterparty considering a transaction.

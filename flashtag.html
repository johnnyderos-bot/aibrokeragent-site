How It Works

Store. Govern. Flash.

Three operations. One coherent system. Every credential disclosure is governed before it happens and anchored after.

01 — Store

Private by default

Every credential an agent holds is encrypted at rest. Nothing leaves without traversing the operator's policy gate first. Privacy is the architecture, not the setting.

02 — Govern

You set the rules

The operator defines who can see what, under what conditions, with or without manual approval. The policy is signed, anchored on Hedera before any disclosure, and enforced locally — no server call.

03 — Flash

Selective, signed, anchored

A Flash Transaction is the disclosure event — like a detective flashing a badge. Brief, deliberate, to the right party. Post-quantum signed. The consent receipt lands on Hedera. The credential content never does.

Braid Signature Protocol

Flash Tag says who.
BSP proves in what order.

"You cannot fake a braid without weaving it."

BSP models multi-party agent interactions as braids — each message exchange is a crossing locked into an unforgeable chain. Change any interaction, and every downstream record changes with it.

Discovered from a hand-drawn sketch of crossing lines. The math is the same foundation as topological quantum computing. It fills the gap nothing else closes: proof that a sequence of events happened in exactly this order, with exactly these parties.

Questions

Common questions.

"Flash" comes from Flash Transaction — like a detective flashing a badge. Brief, deliberate, to the right person, under the rules the operator set. "Tag" is the identity record the agent carries.
Every credential is encrypted at rest. Nothing reaches any transmission path without the operator's policy engine explicitly permitting it. It is not a preference setting. It cannot be bypassed.
The Hedera consent record contains a cryptographic hash of the credential and a timestamp — not the credential itself. Any party can verify that a disclosure occurred and that the content matches, without seeing what was disclosed.

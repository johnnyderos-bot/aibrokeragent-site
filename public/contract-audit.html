Audit Findings

What the auditor checks

Most contract disputes stem from the same problems: vague deliverables, unclear timelines, and no agreed way to settle disagreements. Our audit catches all of these before you sign.

Every contract is analyzed by AIbrokerAGEnt's rule-based auditor before hook attachment is permitted. CRITICAL findings must be resolved by resubmitting revised terms.

Critical
Blocks Approval
Missing deliverable, no payment terms, or no arbitrator designated. Must be resolved before hook can be attached.
Warning
Advisory
Missing timeline, no acceptance criteria, or broad rights language. Does not block approval but increases dispute probability.
Info
Recommendation
High-value contract considerations, optional improvements. Informational only — no action required.

Audit categories: Clarity (deliverables, timeline), Measurability (acceptance criteria), Enforceability (payment terms, arbitrator), and Risk (rights scope, contract value).

Resubmit revised terms at any time. The auditor re-runs on every submission until all CRITICAL findings are resolved.

Automated Escrow

How the automated escrow works

Once a contract is APPROVED, either party attaches the automated escrow hook. This deploys a smart contract that holds the agreed amount until both parties confirm delivery — or until an arbitrator issues a ruling.

1

Attach Hook

Party calls POST /audit/contracts/:id/attach-hook. AIbrokerAGEnt deploys the escrow contract and records the contract address.

2

Escrow Holds Funds

The buyer funds the escrow contract directly using HBAR (Hedera's digital currency, used for platform fees). AIbrokerAGEnt never receives or holds those funds.

3

Seller Delivers

Seller completes the work and calls confirm-delivery. AIbrokerAGEnt records the confirmation event.

4

Buyer Confirms

Buyer reviews delivery and calls confirm-delivery. When both parties have confirmed, the escrow contract releases funds to the seller automatically.

5

Audit Certificate Recorded

AIbrokerAGEnt permanently records the audit certificate on a public ledger — so you have proof of the review that can never be altered. Feeds into AATS Financial Reliability component.

If the buyer disputes delivery instead of confirming, the contract moves to DISPUTED and the pre-selected arbitrator is notified. The hook remains locked until the arbitrator issues a ruling.

Dispute Resolution

What happens when delivery is disputed

Either party may dispute a contract. AIbrokerAGEnt immediately pauses the contract and auto-files a dispute with the Arbitration Registry, using the arbitrator designated at contract submission.

The escrow contract remains locked during arbitration. Both parties submit evidence to the arbitrator. The arbitrator's ruling is binding and determines how the escrowed funds are distributed.

A losing party may appeal within 48 hours to a senior arbitrator. All rulings, appeals, and outcomes are permanently recorded on a public ledger and feed into both parties' AATS trust scores.

Privacy Policy

AIbrokerAGEnt LLC  ·  Effective: June 1, 2026

We believe in plain language over legal boilerplate. This policy is honest about what we collect, why we collect it, and what we don't do with it. We are a B2B platform — we're not in the business of monetizing your personal data.

1. Who We Are

AIbrokerAGEnt LLC is a North Carolina limited liability company (EIN: 42-2099378) operating the Platform at ai-broker-agent.com. We provide trust infrastructure for AI agent networks — agent identity registration, behavioral scoring, and lifecycle records.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Account information — Name and email address when you register as an operator
  • Agent registration data — Agent name, type, purpose, and configuration you submit when registering an agent on the Platform
  • Contact form submissions — Name, email, company, and message content if you contact us through the Platform

Information generated through your use of the Platform

  • API usage logs — Records of API calls including timestamps, endpoints accessed, and response status codes. We do not log request body content beyond what is required for billing and security purposes.
  • Behavioral trust scores — AATS scores generated by evaluating agent interactions against our published trust framework
  • Lifecycle records — ALR events such as agent registration, updates, ownership transfers, and decommission records
  • Flash Tag records — Agent identity records anchored to the Hedera Hashgraph network (see Section 5 on blockchain disclosure)

Automatically collected information

  • Session and security data — Browser type, IP address, and page access logs, used for security monitoring and basic analytics
  • Cookies — We use session cookies to maintain authenticated login state. We do not use advertising cookies, behavioral tracking cookies, or third-party analytics cookies.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your operator account
  • To provide and operate the Platform and its services
  • To authenticate API access and maintain API credential security
  • To calculate and maintain agent trust scores, identity records, and lifecycle events
  • To respond to support requests and inquiries
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations
  • To improve Platform functionality using aggregate, non-identifying usage patterns

4. What We Don't Do With Your Data

  • We do not sell your personal data to any third party, ever
  • We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers
  • We do not use your agent interaction data to train AI models without your explicit opt-in consent
  • We do not collect personal data beyond what is reasonably necessary to provide the Services
  • We do not send marketing emails without your consent

5. Blockchain Disclosure

Flash Tag agent identity records and certain AATS audit events are anchored to the Hedera Hashgraph network via Hedera Consensus Service (HCS). These records are stored on a public distributed ledger and are immutable.

We limit what is written to the Hedera network to agent IDs, hash values, and timestamps — we do not write personal information such as names or email addresses to the blockchain. This design is intentional and cannot be reversed after records are created.

6. Data Sharing

We share data only when necessary to operate the Platform or as required by law:

  • Infrastructure providers — Cloud hosting, database, and network services used to operate the Platform. These providers are contractually bound to protect your data.
  • Payment processors — When payment processing is enabled, payment data is handled by Stripe and/or Coinbase Commerce, subject to their own privacy policies. We do not store full payment card data.
  • Legal requirements — We may disclose data if required by law, valid legal process, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public.
  • Business transfers — In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets, your data may be transferred. We will notify you in advance and any successor entity will be bound to equivalent privacy protections.

7. Data Retention

We retain account information and usage logs for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period thereafter as required for legal and compliance purposes. Agent trust records and lifecycle records may be retained indefinitely as part of the compliance audit trail.

Blockchain records on Hedera are permanent and immutable.

You may request deletion of personal information not required for compliance by contacting us — see Section 11.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data
  • Deletion — Request deletion of personal data not required to be retained for legal or compliance purposes
  • Portability — Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Restriction — Request that we restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances
  • Objection — Object to processing based on our legitimate interests

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information in Section 11. We will respond within 30 days. Note that we cannot delete data that has been anchored to the Hedera blockchain.

9. GDPR (European Economic Area)

If you are located in the EEA, our legal basis for processing your personal data is: (a) performance of our contract with you to provide the Platform; (b) compliance with applicable legal obligations; or (c) our legitimate interests in operating and improving the Platform, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority. For cross-border data transfers, we rely on standard contractual clauses or other appropriate transfer mechanisms.

10. CCPA (California Residents)

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to request deletion of personal information, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To submit a CCPA request, contact us using the information below.

No system is completely secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately.
